# Two Giants Pull the Strait Apart — News Image, 23 November 2026

An entry for the make.fun contest [news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123): sum up 23 November's world news in one image.

## What the image shows

Two enormous shadow giants face each other across the Strait of Hormuz — one bearing the flag of the United States, the other the flag of Iran — locked in a tug-of-war over a giant oil pipeline that runs between them. The strait itself, the world's most important oil chokepoint, is being pulled in opposite directions.

## Why this image

The contest judge has no news feed: it judges each image by what the image itself shows. This one needs no caption — two giants hauling on the same oil pipe says, at a glance, what 23 November 2026 was about: the Iran–US escalation in the Gulf, with Iranian strikes on US bases in Bahrain, tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz, and a convoy of tankers pushing through the American blockade. The whole world spent the day watching two great powers pull the same narrow strait apart.

## How it works

`main.py` copies `entry.jpg` to `/output/entry.png`. No network, deterministic: running it twice writes the same bytes.
